What you get

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Basic Reader strips RSS reading down to what matters: fast, clean access to the content you follow — without any of the bloat, dark patterns, or monthly bills that have crept into the category.

⚡️

Background Auto-Refresh

Your feeds update automatically on a configurable schedule, subject to iOS limitations. No manual refreshing required.

🗂

Folders & Organization

Group feeds into collapsible folders with drag-and-drop reordering. Unread counts show at a glance on every feed and folder.

🔍

Powerful Filter Rules

Hide the noise with wildcard or regex filters, applied globally, per folder, or per feed — against titles, summaries, or both. A filtered indicator shows when rules are active.

📥

OPML Import & Export

Bring your existing subscriptions in from any reader. Take them out just as easily. Your data is never held hostage.

🖥

iPhone, iPad & Mac

One purchase, three platforms. A full three-column layout on iPad and Mac; a clean adaptive UI on iPhone.

🔔

Unread Badge Count

Your app icon shows exactly how many unread articles are waiting — so you never lose track.

⭐️

Stars & Favorites

Star any article to save it for later. Your starred articles stay accessible across every feed and folder.

👆

Swipe Gestures

Swipe to mark articles read or unread without opening them. Accidentally marked everything read? Shake to undo.

↕️

Sort & View Controls

Sort newest or oldest first. Toggle read articles out of view to focus on what's fresh. Pull to refresh any time.

♿️

Accessibility First

Full VoiceOver support, Dynamic Type scaling, and Reduce Motion respect — built in from the start, not bolted on after.

☁️

iCloud Sync

Feeds, folders, filter rules, read status, starred articles, and preferences sync automatically across all your devices via iCloud — no account required beyond your Apple ID.

🔎

Semantic Search

Search across all your articles instantly. Results are re-ranked using an on-device BERT model for semantic relevance — so you find what you mean, not just what you typed. Nothing leaves your device.
