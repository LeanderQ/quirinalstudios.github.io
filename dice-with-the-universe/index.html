Features

Strategy hiding inside every roll

Farkle is deceptively simple: push your luck or bank your points. The catch? Every opponent has a different risk appetite — and so do their dice.

🎲

Physics Dice

Every roll is real. Dice tumble, bounce, and settle using full 3D physics — no animations, no predetermined outcomes.

🌍

15 Periods of History

From the ancient shamans of the Origins era to the rogue AI of the Reckoning. Each period brings new opponents and visual themes.

⚔️

Boss Fights

Each period ends with a boss who plays harder and smarter. Beat them, and claim one of their dice as your own.

⚄

Build Your Collection

Unlock D4s, D8s, D12s, D20s, and more across your journey. Mix and match six dice into your personal loadout.

🎵

Era Soundtrack

Music and visual themes shift as you move through history — from ceremonial drums to synthesized tension.

🧠

Adaptive AI

Each opponent has their own risk tolerance and banking threshold. Genghis Khan plays very differently than Leonardo da Vinci.

The Journey

Three eras. Twenty-seven ways to end the story.

Your choices across 15 periods shape three narrative axes — Order, Voice, Legacy — each with its own reckoning at the end.

I
Origins

The First Games

Sit across the table from a Shaman, a Grain Keeper, Hammurabi, Akhenaten, and Qin Shi Huang. Civilization is young and the stakes are everything. Axis: Order vs. Chaos.

II
Expansion

The Age of Ambition

Caesar, Hypatia, Genghis Khan, Zheng He, and da Vinci. The world is getting bigger — and so is what you stand to lose. Axis: Voice vs. Silence.

III
Reckoning

The Final Roll

Louverture, Tesla, Franklin, an AI Architect, and the Last Council. The universe is watching. What will your dice say about you? Axis: Legacy vs. Oblivion.
