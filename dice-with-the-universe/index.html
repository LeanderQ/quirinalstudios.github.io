Features

Strategy hiding inside every roll

Farkle is deceptively simple: push your luck or bank your points. The catch? Every opponent has a different risk appetite — and so do their dice.

🎲

Physics Dice

Every roll is real. Dice tumble, bounce, and settle using full 3D physics — no canned animations, no predetermined outcomes.

🌍

15 Periods of History

From the Ancient Savanna, c. 50,000 BCE, to the far future. Sixty matches, each period with its own look and music.

⚔️

Boss Fights

Each period ends with a boss who plays harder and smarter. Beat them, and claim one of their dice as your own.

⚄

Build Your Collection

Win a themed die from each of the 15 bosses, from the Spirit Die to the Cosmic Die. Choose six for your Dice Set.

📐

New Dice, New Rules

Each era brings a new die shape, from d4 to d20, and new scoring rules. What pays on a d6 won't pay on a d12, so your strategy has to evolve.

🧠

Opponents With Personality

Each opponent has their own risk tolerance and banking threshold. Genghis Khan plays very differently than Leonardo da Vinci.

📜

Your Choices Matter

Every boss poses a choice. Your answers add up to an archetype for each era, and your three archetypes decide how the story ends.

🎵

The Sound of Each Era

A different music track for every period, with table surfaces and dice sounds that change as you move through history.

✋

Play Your Way

Left-handed layout, shake to roll, haptics, adjustable opponent speed, and a short-game option. Matches save automatically, so you can pick up where you left off.

The Journey

Three eras. Twenty-seven ways to end the story.

Each era tracks three axes of its own. Your choices at every boss settle you into one of three archetypes per era, and the three you earn decide which of 27 endings you reach.

I
Origins

What are we?

Sit across the table from the First Shaman, the Grain Keeper, Hammurabi, Akhenaten, and Qin Shi Huang. Civilization is young and the stakes are everything.

Your axes: Order, Sacred, Boundary.

II
Expansion

Who rules us?

Julius Caesar, Hypatia of Alexandria, Genghis Khan, Zheng He, and Leonardo da Vinci. The world is getting bigger — and so is what you stand to lose.

Your axes: Voice, Knowledge, Kinship.

III
Reckoning

What do we do with our own power?

Toussaint Louverture, Nikola Tesla, Rosalind Franklin, Claudia the AI Architect, and the Last Council. The universe is watching. What will your dice say about you?

Your axes: Agency, Horizon, Legacy.
