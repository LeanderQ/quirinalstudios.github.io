Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Quirinal Studios ("we", "us", or "our") builds apps that respect your privacy. This policy explains what data our apps and website collect, how it is used, and your rights.

Data we collect

Our apps do not collect personally identifiable information. We do not require account registration and do not sell your data to third parties.

Our apps contain no analytics tracking. The only diagnostic information we receive is anonymous crash reports, sent through Apple's system if you choose to share them, which we use to fix problems.

Third-party services

Our apps may use Apple's standard frameworks, which are subject to Apple's own privacy policy.

Our website

This section covers quirinalstudios.com only, not our apps. We use HeyCatch, an analytics service, to see how people find and use the site. It only collects data if you choose Allow when we ask. We remember your choice in your browser, and you can change it at any time.

If you allow it, HeyCatch collects the pages you view; what you click, including the text of links and buttons; the site or campaign that brought you here; details about your device and browser; your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location; and a random identifier. It does not record your screen, and we do not sell this data.

HeyCatch stores that identifier in a cookie, which lasts up to a year, and in your browser's storage, so that return visits can be recognized. Declining after you have allowed it stops collection and removes these. You can also delete or block them in your browser settings, or use a content blocker, and the site will still work.

HeyCatch processes this data for us, including in the United States, using its own service providers. It is kept while we use the service and is deleted or anonymized within two months after our subscription ends. Its code is delivered through esm.sh, which receives your IP address when a page loads, whatever you choose. See HeyCatch's privacy policy. To ask about or delete data about your visits, contact us using the address below.

Data retention

Any data stored by our apps lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app, or is stored in your iCloud account. Our apps do not store personal data on our servers; we don't even have servers. For website data, see "Our website" above.

Children's privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions? Reach us at hello@quirinalstudios.com.
