Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

Quirinal Studios ("we", "us", or "our") builds apps that respect your privacy. This policy explains what data our apps collect, how it is used, and your rights.

Data we collect

Our apps do not collect personally identifiable information. We do not require account registration and do not sell your data to third parties.

Some apps may collect anonymous crash reports and usage analytics to help us improve the experience. This data cannot be used to identify you.

Third-party services

Our apps may use Apple's standard frameworks, which are subject to Apple's own privacy policy.

Data retention

Any data stored by our apps lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. We do not store personal data on our servers.

Children's privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
