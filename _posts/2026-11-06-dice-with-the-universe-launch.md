Dice With the Universe, a Farkle strategy campaign for iPhone, is available now on the App Store. Roll against 15 opponents drawn from history and beyond, from the First Shaman and Hammurabi to Hypatia of Alexandria, Genghis Khan, Nikola Tesla, and the Last Council, across 15 periods of human civilization.

It's free to start: the full tutorial and all of Period 1, including the boss fight, are free. A single one-time purchase unlocks Periods 2 through 15 and Quick Game. No subscription, no ads.

[Read the full press release](/press/dice-with-the-universe-launch/) or [get Dice With the Universe on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6781077479).
