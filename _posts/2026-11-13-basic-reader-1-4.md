Basic Reader 1.4 is out now. Two new ways to get through your reading faster, plus a calmer unread list.

### Full Articles

Some feeds only send a summary and a link. Basic Reader can now fetch the full story and show it right in the reader, so you don't have to leave the app. Your feed's version shows instantly and the full story swaps in as soon as it's ready; if the page has nothing more than the feed, you'll see a note saying so.

- Switch any article between Full Article and Feed Version with a link on the article, or choose your default in Settings under Open Articles With.
- When Full Article is your default, the top unread articles (and the ones next to the one you're reading) are fetched ahead of time.
- Privacy stays intact: the page is read in an isolated, throwaway session, so none of its scripts or trackers ever load.

### Mark as read on scroll

Turn it on and articles you scroll past are marked read for you, in one batch, once the last one has been off screen for a few seconds. Scroll back up and it's cancelled. It's off by default.

### A steadier list

- With Hide Read on, articles marked read in bulk (mark older/newer, or scrolling) now stay where they are until the list refreshes, instead of vanishing and making the list jump.
- Those bulk changes can be undone with a shake.
- Fixed the lead image showing twice at the top of some full articles.

Full Articles and Mark as Read on Scroll are part of the one-time Full unlock.
