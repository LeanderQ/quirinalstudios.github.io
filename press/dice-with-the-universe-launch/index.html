Quirinal Studios Releases Dice With the Universe, a Physics-Based Farkle Campaign Through 15 Periods of Human History

Toronto, Ontario — November 6, 2026

Quirinal Studios, the independent iOS studio behind Basic Reader, today announced the release of Dice With the Universe, a physics-based Farkle strategy campaign for iPhone. Players roll real, physics-driven dice against 15 opponents drawn from history and beyond — from the First Shaman and Hammurabi to Hypatia of Alexandria, Genghis Khan, Nikola Tesla, and the Last Council — across 15 periods of human civilization. The game is available now on the App Store as a free download, with a single one-time purchase to unlock the full campaign — no subscription, no ads.

Farkle is a simple dice game with a hard question at its core: bank your points, or risk everything for one more roll? Dice With the Universe builds a full campaign around that decision. Every opponent has their own risk tolerance and banking threshold, so Genghis Khan plays very differently than Leonardo da Vinci, and each period ends with a boss who plays harder and smarter.

"Farkle is easy to learn and hard to put down, because every roll is a gamble and every opponent has their own idea of risk. I wanted to wrap that in a journey through human history, and to ship it the way I shipped Basic Reader: play the first period free, pay once for the rest, and no subscription, ever." — Leander Quiring, founder of Quirinal Studios

Highlights

  • 15 periods across three eras — Origins, Expansion, and Reckoning — each with its own look and music
  • Boss fights that award a themed die; win one from each boss and choose six for your dice set
  • New dice shapes, from d4 to d20, bring new scoring rules as the eras advance
  • Narrative choices at every boss shape three archetypes that decide which of 27 endings you reach
  • Play your way — left-handed layout, shake to roll, haptics, adjustable opponent speed, and a short-game option, with matches that save automatically

Free to start, one purchase for the rest

  • Free: the full tutorial and all of Period 1, including the boss fight and its narrative choice
  • One-time unlock: Periods 2 through 15, and Quick Game, which replays any boss you've beaten with any dice you've unlocked

Progress syncs across a player's devices via iCloud. There is no account to create, and no ads or tracking.

Dice With the Universe is available today on the App Store for iPhone.
