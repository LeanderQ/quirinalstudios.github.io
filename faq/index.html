FAQ

Answers to common questions about our apps.

General

What platforms are your apps available on?

Our apps are built for iOS. We don't currently have plans for Android or web versions.

Are your apps free?

Our apps aren't free, but we all have subscription fatigue. We have no plans to ever offer subscriptions for our apps, but we may paywall new features to fund their development. We will never paywall or remove existing features unless not doing to would break the app. If GenAI features are ever added, we'll provide the option for you to use your own API keys.

Do you use generative AI in your apps or development?

Yes — we use generative AI tools in ideation, coding, debugging, and content writing. We're aware of the genuine concerns people have around GenAI, and we don't take them lightly. But we also want to be honest: these apps simply wouldn't exist without it. Quirinal Studios is one person building in the hours after the kids go to sleep. GenAI is what makes that possible at all.

Where can I find the latest updates?

Updates are released through the App Store. Enable automatic updates on your device to always have the latest version.

Is the art in Dice With the Universe AI-generated?
